<Note>
  **Tired of your team forgetting the most important rule in ops?** No, not "never deploy on a Friday". The *other* one: **3-2-1**. Three copies of your data, on two kinds of storage, with one offsite.
</Note>

NoBackups is named after the situation it gets you out of. It's one small static binary you drop on a Linux server. You tell it what matters and where to send it, and it handles the rest on a schedule. That way "so… does anyone have a backup?" stays a hypothetical, not something posted in the team chat at 3 AM.

```yaml /etc/nobackups/config.yaml
destinations:
  hetzner:
    type: hetzner
    region: fsn1
    bucket: my-backups
    access_key_id: ${HETZNER_ACCESS_KEY}
    secret_access_key: ${HETZNER_SECRET_KEY}
    prefix: servers/{hostname}

jobs:
  - name: system
    sources: [/etc, /home, /root]
    destinations: [hetzner]
    schedule: "15 3 * * *"
    encryption:
      passphrase: ${BACKUP_PASSPHRASE}
    retention: { keep_last: 7, keep_days: 30 }
```

## What's in the box

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Any S3-compatible storage" icon="bucket">
    Hetzner, RustFS, MinIO, Garage, Alarik, AWS, Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, plus local disks and NFS. Your data, your bucket list.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Many destinations, one pass" icon="arrows-split-up-and-left">
    Each archive is built once and streamed to every destination in parallel. If one destination fails, the rest still finish.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Streams, never stages" icon="water">
    Nothing is written to local disk first. Light enough for even the most *byte*-sized VPS.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compressed & encrypted" icon="lock">
    zstd or gzip, then age encryption with a passphrase or public keys. Squeezed, sealed, delivered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Retention" icon="broom">
    Keep the last N snapshots and/or everything newer than N days. Old snapshots are let go gracefully.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hooks" icon="link">
    Dump databases or pause containers before a backup, and clean up after.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discord & webhooks" icon="bell">
    Colour-coded alerts on failure, warning or success, globally or per job.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Boring to operate" icon="server">
    systemd daemon, cron schedules, locking, a status command and config reload. Boring is a feature.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
